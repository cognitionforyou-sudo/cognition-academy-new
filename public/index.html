About Cognition Academy

Dear Parents,

We at Cognition Academy are committed to provide quality education in a highly competitive, congenial and healthy environment along with the best infrastructure. We infuse exemplary skills that are demanded from the students to be successful in IIT-JEE, BITSAT, AIIMS, NEET and other major competitive examinations.

Here at Cognition Academy, we truly understand that the final outcome is always in the hands of student; but, we also believe that it is the teacher who can truly direct the student to achieve the desired goal. I take personal interest in what's going on in my classes, to confirm that we as an organisation achieve the desired outcome.

I always believe that each one of us can shine in whatever we undertake. Our faculty is there to mould your desire, dedication and determination to the path of success.

I, on behalf of entire Cognition Academy team welcome you to join us and feel the blend of quality & Innovation that can change you to be the world class professional.

— Mr. Kunal Dolas, Director, Cognition Academy, Wardha


About Us: COGNITION Institute is for the aspirants of IIT-JEE/NEET competitive exams. COGNITION is empowered with a team of dedicated and expert faculties hail from NAVODAYA focussed on preparing the students for most challenging and rigorous competitive exams and also to pioneer them to achieve success in these examinations. We believe in providing an additional edge to all our IIT-JEE/NEET aspirants by framing a strong foundation for their preparation.

Our Vision & Mission

  • Providing all deserving students in Vidarbha with the best curriculum and pedagogy along with the highest quality of education in nurturing the students for board and entrance examination.
  • Making learning process rudimentary strong, imaginative and enjoyable using innovative methods of teaching.
  • Strongly believing in implementing students self-learning approach with our proper guidance.
  • Training every aspirant on NAVODAYA's teaching line.

Salient Features

  • Highly dynamic environment of competitive exams
  • Structural units of syllabus
  • Cumulative tests and model tests
  • Chapter/topic wise preparation with past year papers
  • Special revision classes especially for weak aspirants
  • Clarity of concepts instead of mere dictation
  • Time to time career counseling and guidance
